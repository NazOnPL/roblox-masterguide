## 6. The Roblox Discovery Algorithm & Launch Blueprint

### The Sponsored Ads Launch Formula
- Budget: **16 Ad Credits ($16) per day**.
- Targeting: Select **All Ages / Recommended**.
- Duration: Run continuously for **3 to 7 days**.
- Test in **Beta Mode** to optimize funnels before entering public home recommendations.

### The 500 Highly Engaged Players Threshold
- A highly engaged player is any account that has made a financial purchase anywhere on Roblox within the past 60 days.
- Initial ad traffic will feature slow growth due to platform bots.
- As funnels optimize, organic impressions kick in and the threshold accelerates from 100 to 500 within days.

### The Ad Cutoff Rule
- Monitor Creator Dashboard impressions daily.
- **Turn off paid ads the exact moment Home Recommendation impressions surpass Sponsored Ad impressions.**
