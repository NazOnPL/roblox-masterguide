# Roll a Racer: playtest notes (2026-10-24)

Naz on an alt account, PC, fresh save, played up to Sandy Shores. Fix list: fix-list.md. Ideas: ideas.md.

## What works (keep it)

- Boss race chase camera. Camera right behind the car, seeing the cars you pass. This was the most fun part of the session.
- Garage menu pop-out tween. The reference for every other menu.
- Index: 50 cars, silhouettes, Gold (1 in 50) and Rainbow (1 in 1,000) shines, first-find cash, row bonus for luck and cash. Solid collection layer.
- Area 2 (Sandy Shores) feels open and free to move around.
- Nameplates in the Locker.

## What doesn't (see fix-list.md for every item)

- Normal race is not fun: top-down camera, 5-second laps, tiny track, identical opponent cars, too much bloom, unclear drift on PC.
- 8-second wait before each race.
- Boss is no challenge: player 16s, boss 26s.
- FTUE has friction: spawn not facing the roll button, dim screen that stays, two roll panels, detours to Garage and Skill Tree, long walk to the race.
- UI is static and inconsistent: X buttons misaligned, notifications vanish, small text in Index, cluttered car billboards, prices hidden on hover.
- Stock VFX and native ProximityPrompts make it feel unpolished.

## Naz's verdict

"I don't really see why a kid, if he doesn't like the first couple minutes of the game, would stick around for more." The game needs things that mix up the race (power-ups, themed tracks) and a polish pass so nothing is static or jarring.
