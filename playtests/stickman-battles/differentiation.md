# Stickman Battles: how to stand apart from Stick Wars

Stick Wars (~2,000 CCU) shipped the same premise first. Copying its lobby and features makes us the second version of their game. The goal is a 20% twist that a kid can explain in one sentence: "It's like Stick Wars but ___."

## What we already have that they don't (lean in)

1. **The class triangle.** Brawler > Trapper > Shooter > Brawler. Stick Wars has rarity; we have strategy. This is the strongest hook already built.
2. **Rival Road (PvE ladder).** You can fight CPU rivals right away. Stick Wars needs two real players on the pads. At launch we'll have low CCU, so a no-wait solo path is a real advantage, not a side mode.
3. **Neon look.** Their game is a bright cartoon city. A dark neon arena is instantly recognizable in a thumbnail.

## Big decision: top-down or side-view?

Naz wants stickmen running on obstacles like in Stick Wars. Two ways to get there:

- **A. Keep top-down, drop the balls (recommended).** Stickmen run, dash, dodge and slash in a top-down neon arena with pillars, bounce walls and hazards. Keeps the matches Naz already loves, keeps the Webber's wall-bounce web, and looks nothing like Stick Wars on a screen.
- **B. Switch to side-view platforms.** Closer to the stickman fantasy, but it's their exact look. Every thumbnail and lobby screen would read as "Stick Wars clone".

## Twist ideas (pick 1 or 2, not all)

**Team of 3 with live swapping (Pokémon lens).** Bring all three classes into a match. Tap to swap mid-fight; the swap is the skill (counter-swap when they swap). The triangle stops being a menu and becomes the gameplay. One-sentence pitch: "Stick Wars, but you bring a squad of 3 and counter-swap."

**Neon doodles.** Stickmen are drawn in light. Every fighter is drawn in line by line when the match starts, KOs shatter into neon strokes, and your rival roster are "Doodles" with names and personalities (Rival Doodle already exists). Gives the game its own identity, VFX style and thumbnail.

**King of the Neon (our server event).** Instead of a copy of Server Brawl: one giant center-stage arena, winner stays on, anyone can queue to dethrone the king. The king gets a crown, a coin bonus per win, and their name on the lobby screen. The whole server watches and cheers. Runs every 10 minutes for 3 minutes.

**Crowd powers.** Spectators in the lobby can cheer a fighter (free, with a cooldown) or drop a paid power-up into a match they're watching (e.g. a heal orb that both fighters can grab, so it's fun, not pay-to-win). Turns spectating into playing.

**Arena modifiers.** Each match rolls one rule shown before the fight: low gravity, shrinking ring, laser sweep, mirror arena, double speed. Cheap variety; each one is a chance for a clip.

**Rival Road bosses.** Every 7th rival is a boss doodle with a unique gimmick (giant size, two weapons, summons). Beating one unlocks a stickman or a weapon. Gives the solo path a destination.

**Weapons as their own collection.** Twin Fang is already a named weapon. Separate stickmen (class) from weapons (style): any Brawler can wield any blade. Two collections, more crate variety, more trading.

**2v2 with a friend.** Invite a friend, pick classes that cover each other's weakness. Makes the Invite button do something better than a free crate.

## Packaging

"Stickman Battles" and "Stick Wars" read almost the same in search. Worth testing a name and thumbnail that say the twist (squad, neon, doodles), once the twist is chosen. Use the validate-concept skill for the title check.
