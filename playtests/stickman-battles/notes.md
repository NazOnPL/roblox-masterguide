# Stickman Battles: playtest notes (2026-10-25)

Naz's game (stickman-vs-stickman project), about 80 to 90% done. First playtest on a fresh alt, PC, in Studio. Played side by side with the competitor Stick Wars (`../../competitors/stick-wars.md`, ~2,000 CCU, same premise, shipped first).
Fix list: fix-list.md. Differentiation: differentiation.md. Screenshots: `meet-your-stickmen.png`, `arena-lobby-view.png`, `match-hud.png`.

## What the game is today (from the screenshots)

- You get three stickmen, one per class, in a rock-paper-scissors triangle: Brawlers beat Trappers, Trappers beat Shooters, Shooters beat Brawlers ("Meet your stickmen": Slasher, Archer, Webber).
- Matches are top-down in a neon square arena with a grid floor. Each stickman sits in a circle (ball-vs-ball style) with HP and a stat badge (e.g. Twin Fang, SPIN 15.5, Lv1).
- PvE ladder "Rival Road" against CPU rivals ("2/7 Your box is ready! Fight Rival Doodle"), so you can play without a second player.
- Neon synthwave look (cyan/purple/yellow walls, dark grid).

## What works (keep it)

- The matches themselves. "It's actually pretty awesome how my matches play out. It's really fun."
- Slow-mo when someone is about to win. Keep and maybe push further (zoom, hit-stop on the final blow).
- The neon vibe. Keep the direction, fix readability.

## What doesn't (see fix-list.md)

- Too much text on the first screens.
- No reveal for your first three stickmen. Stick Wars rolls them in.
- Stickmen are stuck inside balls. Naz wants them to run, jump, dodge, block and use obstacles like real stickmen.
- Aim relies on mouse position, and pressing the lock-aim button moves the mouse and ruins the aim.
- UI looks generic ("AI slop"). Needs custom art like his other games.
- Neon is hard to see. The circular shape isn't the look he wants.
- Pick-your-stickman screen is much less polished than Stick Wars'.

## Naz's verdict

Fun core, same idea as a game that already has 2,000 players. The match loop is good; the gap is polish, the stickman fantasy (movement), and a clear reason to play his instead of Stick Wars.
