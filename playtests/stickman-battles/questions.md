# Stickman Battles: questions to go deeper

Two sets. Part 1 is for Naz to answer (answers go in notes.md / differentiation.md). Part 2 is paste-ready for the stickman-vs-stickman project.

## Part 1: for Naz

**The feel**
1. Top-down with running stickmen and obstacles, or side-view platforms like Stick Wars? (See differentiation.md; top-down is recommended so we don't look like a clone.)
2. When you picture the best moment in a match, what is it? A clutch dodge, a combo, a comeback, a big final hit?
3. How long should a match be? Under 60 seconds, or longer fights with back and forth?
4. Do you control your stickman fully (move + attack), or set them up and watch them fight (aim, launch, let them go)? Today it's closer to the second.

**The twist**
5. Which twist excites you most: squad of 3 with swapping, neon doodles, King of the Neon, crowd powers, arena modifiers, weapons as a collection, 2v2?
6. Finish the sentence a kid would say: "It's like Stick Wars but ___."
7. Do you use all three starting stickmen in one match, or pick one per match? What does Stick Wars do?
8. Is the name Stickman Battles locked, or open to change if the twist suggests a better one?

**Players and progress**
9. What does a player chase after day 1? New stickmen, weapons, ranks, Rival Road bosses?
10. Is there a ranked or trophy system? Should there be (it's free retention for PvP)?
11. Can you fight CPU rivals in Stick Wars, or only real players?
12. What's your plan when the server has 3 players? (Rival Road answers this; is it the default Play button target?)

**Money**
13. What do you want people to spend Robux on: crates, specific stickmen, cosmetics (trails, KO effects, nameplates), passes?
14. Any pay-to-win you're okay with (e.g. stat boosts), or cosmetic and convenience only?

**Social and events**
15. What should happen in the lobby while you wait? Watch, bet, cheer, practice?
16. How often do you want an event, and what's the prize?
