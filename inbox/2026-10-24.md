## Fake an Egg (Slime Time Studios), ~29k CCU
Sorted into: competitors/fake-an-egg.md, 04-game-design-retention/notes.md, 03-packaging/notes.md, 05-monetization/notes.md

Raw (Naz, voice): Tutorial removes friction everywhere. After you grab an egg and the next step is "go back to base", the camera is pre-rotated and panned toward your base. Uses cutscenes like a lot of new games. Premise: place an egg in your plot, a big suction cup sucks you up and drops you in the middle of the map where eggs spawn, an NPC picks you up thinking you're an egg and puts you in his area, and now you can steal his pets. After the tutorial it immediately prompts you to sign up for the next event (Roblox native Events / RSVP UI). Right after the tutorial it prompts a rebirth you already qualify for, with rewards; feels like progression. After the tutorial, beams/arrows keep leading you to the next goal. You spawn already holding a free velociraptor egg with a 3-minute timer, which makes you want to see what hatches. Custom proximity prompt for hatching; camera zooms in and shakes during the hatch, suspenseful, then snaps back. Every buy and pickup has VFX + SFX. Pets on the plot show a $/s label that pops in diagonally, slightly oversized, then rotates straight and settles, like it slides in. UI is clean and simple. Thumbnails are great. Gripe: after the tutorial it depends on other players picking you up as an egg; if nobody does, you wasted 30 seconds.
