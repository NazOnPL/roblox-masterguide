# Monetization: Naz's notes

Brain dumps, playtest observations and your own rules for this topic. Newest first.

## 2026-10-24 (from Roll a Racer playtest)

- Store as one long scrolling list with section headers (Passes, Boosts, Packs, Cash) beats tabs. Every pass gets its own icon.
- Keep cosmetics out of the Robux store; they live in the Locker. Odds don't belong in the store either.
- Animated cosmetics (rainbow gradient nameplates, shimmer, trails) are cheap to make and visible to everyone: good flex products and rewards.
- Sell from the moment of pain: offer an instant boss rematch right after a close loss.

## 2026-10-24 (from Fake an Egg)

- Always-visible banner selling offline earnings ("Your pets earn $181.4K/day offline").
- Friend boost % with a + button on the main HUD as an invite pathway.
- Scheduled event countdown ("ADMIN ABUSE: 34H") on screen at all times.
