# Stick Wars

- Genre / loop: 1v1 stickman battles (ball-vs-ball style), collect stickmen from crates, spectate other matches in a shared lobby
- CCU when played: ~2,000 · Date played: 2026-10-25 · Played on: PC
- Why it matters: same premise as Naz's Stickman Battles, and it shipped first. This file is the "what are we up against" reference for differentiation.
- Screenshots: `stick-wars/lobby.png`, `stick-wars/match-pads.png`, `stick-wars/crates.png`, `stick-wars/arena-screen-waiting.png`

## Core loop in one line

Roll stickmen from crates, stand on a red/blue pad to queue a 1v1, watch your stickman fight on the big screen between the pads, earn coins, open more crates.

## What I liked (steal this)

**Lobby**
- Simple, central lobby that matches the vibe: arena screens line a central runway, shop/bundle pads sit along the sides, leaderboard and crates are all in one walkable area.
- Every arena is a big screen with a red pad and a blue pad in front of it. Stand on one, "0/2 PLAYERS, WIN 100 COINS" shows, and the screen says "WAITING, Noob1 wants a fight!". Live matches play on the screens, so the lobby is full of fights to watch (free spectating, free social proof).
- "Challenge" proximity prompt on other players to fight them directly.
- Bundles, VIP, Battle Pass and featured stickmen are physical pads in the world with BUY signs, next to a live display of that stickman.

**HUD**
- Centralized, huge PLAY button with INVITE right next to it. Invite carries a "FREE CRATE" tag. Naz: "those are really good."
- "FREE EPIC STICKMAN 2/5" progress bar above Play (playtime / session reward).
- Daily Quests panel always visible top right (play/invite a friend, break 3 combos, land 25 air hits), with a Trade tab.
- Timers everywhere that give a reason to stay: Founder gift (7:36), 2x Coins (12:37), Candy Rain event (0:19).
- Left rail: Halloween (event), Shop, Stickmen, Pass. Currencies bottom left (candy, coins, gems).

**Server Brawl**
- A scheduled event ("Next Brawl in 8:17", "Survive to the final") where the whole server fights each other, with prizes. A countdown in the lobby builds hype and keeps people in the server.

**FTUE**
- First join plays a roll animation for your first three stickmen: screen split into three horizontal strips, each one spins like an MM2 crate and lands one after another (boom, boom, boom). Naz wants this.
- The "pick your stickman" screen is polished and feels cool. Mostly UI and polish, not mechanics.

**Matches**
- Stickmen run around a 2D side-view stage with little walls/platforms to run and jump on (see `stick-wars/arena-screen-waiting.png`). Feels like stickmen, not balls.
- Aim is click-and-drag (Naz thinks; to confirm).

**Crates**
- Basic / Super / Legend crates, Open x1 (250) and x10 (2,250, so a 10% discount), "What's inside" scroller with per-stickman odds, rarity bar (Rare 70%, Epic 24%, Legendary 5%, Mythic 1%).

## What felt bad (avoid this)

- Matches need two real players on the pads. With low CCU you wait. (Opportunity for us, see twist.)
- Crate UI is a generic tabbed panel.

## Monetization seen

Battle Pass, VIP pad, Founder gift (timed), 2x Coins boost (timed), named stickman bundles on pads (e.g. Riftstorm bundle with hover bike), gems currency with + buttons, crates (coins), seasonal Halloween event with candy currency, trading.
