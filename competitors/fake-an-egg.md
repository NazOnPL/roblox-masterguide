# Fake an Egg

- Studio: Slime Time Studios
- Genre / loop: pet collect + steal, with a disguise-as-an-egg twist
- CCU when played: ~29,000 · Date played: 2026-10-24
- Screenshots: `fake-an-egg/hud-gameplay.png`, `fake-an-egg/thumbnail-1.png`, `fake-an-egg/thumbnail-2.png`

## Core loop in one line

Place an egg in your plot, get sucked up and dropped among the wild eggs disguised as one, get carried into someone else's plot, then steal their pets.

## What I liked (steal this)

**Tutorial with zero friction**
- When the next step is "go back to base", the camera is already rotated and panned toward the base. The player never has to look for where to go.
- Cutscenes carry the premise (suction cup pulls you up, NPC carries you off as an egg). The concept is taught by showing it, not by text.
- After the tutorial ends, Beam arrows keep pointing at the next goal.

**Instant progression right after the tutorial**
- The game prompts a rebirth you already qualify for, and it pays rewards. You feel progress in the first few minutes.
- It immediately prompts you to RSVP to the next event with Roblox's native Events UI. That's a free return trigger for the next update drop.

**A free reward on a timer**
- You spawn already holding a velociraptor egg with a 3-minute hatch timer. You did nothing to earn it, and you stay to see what hatches.

**Juice everywhere**
- Custom-styled proximity prompts (see the "Disguise" prompt).
- Hatch is a mini cutscene: camera zooms in, shakes slightly, then snaps back.
- Every purchase and pickup has VFX and a sound.
- Each pet's $/s billboard pops in tilted and slightly oversized, then rotates straight and shrinks to size with a small slide.

## What felt bad (avoid this)

- After the tutorial (NPC-driven), getting carried depends on real players picking you up. If nobody does, you wasted ~30 seconds. A core loop step should never hinge on another player choosing to act; give it an NPC or timeout fallback.

## HUD notes (from hud-gameplay.png)

- Top center banner: "Your pets earn $181.4K/day offline". Sells the offline-earnings fantasy constantly.
- Top right: "ADMIN ABUSE: 34H 33M" countdown. A scheduled live event always on screen.
- Left stack: big cash number, friend boost "+0%" with a + button (invite pathway), a timer, and a "Reveal Fakes" button.
- Right stack: Pets (2/7 capacity), Index, Rebirth. Three buttons only.
- Bottom right: next weather in 3:48. Another reason to stay.
- Plot: each pet shows name, rarity (Rainbow mutation tag), $/s. A "x1.1 Pet Cash" pad shows when it runs empty (0:20:52).
- Hotbar has just 2 items.

## Monetization seen

- Friend-boost + button, Index, rebirth rewards. (Not explored further yet.)

## FTUE (first 90 seconds)

Grab egg, camera auto-points to base, place egg, cutscene sucks you into the egg field, NPC carries you to its plot, steal pets, rebirth prompt with rewards, event RSVP prompt, Beam to next goal. Free timed egg in hand throughout.

## Packaging (title, thumbnail, icon)

- Title is the `[Verb] a [Noun]` formula: "Fake an Egg".
- Both thumbnails: a bacon-hair character's face peeking out of a cracked egg among plain white eggs, a red arrow, and the word "YOU" in huge white text. One idea, readable at phone size, 1 to 2 characters.
- Thumbnail 1 adds a giant hand reaching for the eggs (tension). Thumbnail 2 adds a sleeping player (you're tricking them).

## Our 20% twist idea

- Same disguise fantasy, but the carry step has an NPC fallback so solo players never wait.
