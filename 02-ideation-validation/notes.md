## 2026-10-25 (from validating the $20 auction draft, see ideas/auction-draft.md)

- A TikTok trend gets cloned on Roblox within weeks. By the time you see it everywhere, check how many games already copy it before calling it demand.
- Themed reskins are a lottery on the theme: Choose the Stronger Anime kept growing while the soccer version fell 93% in 6 weeks.
- Pick themes where "who's better" is arguable (anime power-scaling), not looked up (FIFA ratings).
- Copy the part that made the trend work, not just the rules: the TikTok draft works because the comment section judges.
