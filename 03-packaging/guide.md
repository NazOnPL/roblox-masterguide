## 3. Titling & Packaging: The Mobile CTR Engine

### Titling Rules
- Speak at a **3rd Grade reading level** (e.g., "Hack a Business" converts 2x better than "Sell Your Data").
- Use proven action formulas:
  * `[Verb] a [Noun]` (*Build a Base*, *Hack a Business*, *Grow a Garden*)
  * `Be a [Noun]` (*Be a YouTuber*, *Be a Lucky Block*)
  * `Escape the [Noun]` (*Escape Tsunami*)
  * `[Implied Challenge]` (*99 Nights in the Forest*, *Hide from the Villain*)

### High-Converting Thumbnail Guidelines
- **Mobile First (QPTr Tool)**: Test all thumbnails at mobile scale. If elements are unreadable on a phone screen, the thumbnail will flop.
- **1–2 Characters Max**: Avoid crowded scenes.
- **Exaggerated Facial Expressions**: Readable emotions (evil grin, crying, panicked eyes).
- **High Color Contrast**: Punchy primary colors that separate foreground from background.
- **Zero Visual Noise**: Remove background clouds, clutter, and unreadable textures.
- **Tease the Core Loop**: Visually convey the core fantasy and unique action.
- **Volume Testing**: Run campaigns testing 20–50 thumbnails simultaneously.
