# Tree chopping with real cuts

- Clip: `clip.mp4` (57 s). Frames: `frames-1.jpg` to `frames-3.jpg`.

## What it is

First-person axe on low-poly trees. Each swing cuts a wedge-shaped notch into the trunk exactly where the axe lands, with light wood showing inside the cut. The notch deepens with each hit, and wood chips burst out and stay on the ground. Once the cut is deep enough the top of the tree tips over and falls, leaving a stump with a clean, ring-textured cut face. You can cut at different heights and get several notches on one trunk.

## Why it's good

- The world reacts exactly where you hit, so every swing feels like it matters. That's the "every action has instant feedback" rule done properly.
- Chips that stay on the ground and the stump left behind show progress without any UI.
- The fall is a payoff moment worth building a whole loop around.

## Where it could fit

- Any gathering loop (wood, ore, ice, crystals), simulators, survival, or a "Chop a [Thing]" title.
- The leftover stump could regrow on a timer, which gives a reason to come back.
