# Pixel explosion VFX

- Clip: `clip.mp4` (16 s). Frames: `frames.jpg`.

## What it is

A character in an empty baseplate triggers an explosion: a fiery blob (red outside, yellow and light-blue core) plus a separate dark-red smoke puff. Both are drawn as big square pixels, like a 2D pixel-art sprite living in the 3D world. As the camera zooms and orbits, the pixels stay the same chunky size on screen, so it reads as a deliberate retro style rather than a low-res texture.

## Why it's good

- A distinctive look for almost no art cost. A whole game could use it as its visual identity (pixel VFX on normal Roblox geometry).
- Very readable at phone size: flat colours, hard edges, no soft gradients.
- Only ~5 colours, which matches the "pick about 5 colours" rule in the VFX skill.

## Watch out

- Red splats can read as blood in a thumbnail or a still frame. Keep the orange/yellow fire read strong, and avoid red puffs near characters if the game isn't combat.

## Where it could fit

- Explosions, pickups, hit sparks or currency pops in a stylised game. A pixel style could be the 20% twist on a familiar genre.
